# Situ Note

Situ Note publishes personal writing and lets readers browse or search it.

## Language

**Blog**:
The site's collection of dated posts, browsable through an archive and categories.

**Blog post**:
A dated piece of writing in the blog collection. A blog post has a slug and can belong to multiple categories.

**Category**:
A label assigned to a blog post. The same category gathers posts on its own blog route.

**Archive**:
The chronological listing of blog posts, grouped by year.

**Insight**:
A numbered piece of writing in the insight collection, separate from blog posts.

**Search**:
The reader's lookup across the site's indexed writing.

**Reading view**:
The full-text presentation used for blog posts and other long-form site writing.
